Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Resumaiz ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

  • Resume content: Text you paste into our tool is sent to Anthropic's API for processing and is never stored on our servers.
  • Email address: If you voluntarily provide your email, we use it only to send product updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Payment information: All payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Local storage: Your profile, applications, and preferences are stored in your browser's local storage — on your device only.
  • Usage analytics: We may collect anonymous usage data (page views, feature usage) to improve the product. No personally identifiable information is included.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the resume rewriting and job search services
  • To send product updates if you've opted in with your email
  • To process payments securely via Stripe
  • To improve the product based on anonymous usage patterns

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • Anthropic: Resume text is processed by Anthropic's Claude API under their privacy policy. Text is not used to train their models.
  • Stripe: Payment processing only.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law.

Data Retention

Resume content is processed in real-time and not stored on our servers. Profile data is stored in your browser's local storage and can be deleted by clearing your browser data at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal information we hold. Contact us at hello@resumaiz.com with any requests.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies necessary for the application to function. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@resumaiz.com.
